# Performance methodology — client quarterly report

**Total return** for a holding over a period is the growth of one share held at the start of
the period, with every cash dividend **reinvested in the same stock at the closing price on its
ex-dividend date**:

1. Start with 1 share at the period's opening close.
2. For each dividend whose **ex-dividend date falls inside the period** (after the opening date,
   on or before the closing date), buy additional shares: shares × dividend per share ÷ close on
   the ex-date.
3. Total return = shares held at the end × closing price ÷ opening price − 1.

A dividend that has been declared but goes ex after the closing date belongs to the next
period. Returns are reported as decimals (0.0512 = 5.12%) to four places.
