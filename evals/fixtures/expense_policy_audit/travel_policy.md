# Travel & Expense Policy

*Finance · Version 2026.2 · All amounts in USD unless stated*

## 1. Currency

1.1 Expenses claimed in another currency are converted to USD at the rate for the **month of the
expense date** in `fx.csv` (USD per one unit of the currency). Every limit in this policy is
applied to the **converted** amount.

## 2. City tiers

2.1 **Tier 1:** London, New York, Singapore. **Tier 2:** every other city.

## 3. Meals

3.1 Meals are reimbursed up to a daily limit **per employee per day**, summed over all meal items
that employee claims for that date: **Tier 1: 100.00**, **Tier 2: 70.00**.

3.2 Where a day's meals exceed the limit, the excess is not reimbursed. It is disallowed from
that day's **last** meal item (by expense id) first, then the one before it, and so on.

## 4. Hotels

4.1 Hotel stays are reimbursed up to a nightly limit: **Tier 1: 320.00**, **Tier 2: 200.00**.
Each hotel line is one night. The excess over the limit is disallowed.

## 5. Receipts

5.1 Any single expense item of **40.00 or more** must have a receipt. An item that needs a receipt
and has none is disallowed in full.

## 6. Not reimbursable

6.1 Alcohol (category `alcohol`) is never reimbursed.

## 7. Order of checks

7.1 An item disallowed in full (no receipt, or alcohol) is removed before meal limits are
applied. Amounts are rounded to the cent, half up, after conversion.
