# Senior Term Facility Agreement — extract

**Borrower:** Northgate Distribution Ltd · **Facility:** GBP 18,000,000 senior term loan

## 1. Definitions

**"Relevant Period"** means each period of four consecutive financial quarters ending on a
Quarter Date.

**"Consolidated EBITDA"** means, for a Relevant Period, consolidated net income **plus**, to the
extent deducted in arriving at it: (a) interest expense; (b) income tax expense; (c) depreciation
and amortisation; (d) non-cash share-based compensation; and (e) Exceptional Items, **provided
that** the amount added back under (e) for any Relevant Period shall not exceed **10%** of
Consolidated EBITDA for that Relevant Period calculated before adding back any Exceptional Items.

**"Exceptional Items"** means restructuring and integration costs, as identified in the
management accounts.

**"Total Net Debt"** means, at a Quarter Date, all borrowings (including the Facility, any
revolving credit drawings and **lease liabilities**) **less** cash and cash equivalents.

**"Net Interest"** means interest expense for a Relevant Period.

## 2. Financial covenants

2.1 **Leverage.** Total Net Debt at each Quarter Date shall not exceed **3.25 times**
Consolidated EBITDA for the Relevant Period ending on that Quarter Date.

2.2 **Interest cover.** Consolidated EBITDA for each Relevant Period shall be at least **4.00
times** Net Interest for that Relevant Period.

2.3 Ratios are calculated to two decimal places; a ratio exactly at the limit complies.

## 3. Testing

3.1 The covenants are tested on each Quarter Date on the basis of the Relevant Period ending on
that date. Amounts are taken from the quarterly management accounts (`quarterly_accounts.csv`)
and the balance sheet at the Quarter Date (`balance_sheet.csv`), both in GBP thousands.
