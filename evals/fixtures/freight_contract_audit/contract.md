# Parcel Rate Agreement

**Between** Northgate Distribution Ltd ("Shipper") **and** SwiftLine Express ("Carrier")
**Agreement no.** SL-2026-114 · **Term** 1 July 2026 – 30 June 2027

## 1. Chargeable weight

1.1 Each shipment is billed on its chargeable weight: the greater of its actual weight and its
volumetric weight.

1.2 Volumetric weight (kg) = length × width × height (cm) ÷ **6,000**.

1.3 Chargeable weight is rounded **up** to the next 0.5 kg.

## 2. Base charge

2.1 The base charge is the rate for the first 0.5 kg plus the rate for each additional 0.5 kg of
chargeable weight, by destination zone:

| Zone | First 0.5 kg | Each additional 0.5 kg |
|---|---:|---:|
| A | 6.00 | 1.20 |
| B | 8.00 | 1.60 |
| C | 11.00 | 2.10 |

2.2 **Minimum charge.** The base charge of a shipment is never less than **9.00**.

## 3. Fuel surcharge

3.1 A fuel surcharge applies to the **base charge only**. It is not applied to any accessorial
charge.

3.2 The surcharge rate is set monthly and applies by the shipment's **ship date**:

| Month | Rate |
|---|---:|
| September 2026 | 12.5% |
| October 2026 | 14.0% |

## 4. Accessorial charges

4.1 **Residential delivery:** 3.50 per shipment delivered to a residential address.

## 5. Billing

5.1 All amounts are in GBP. Each charge component (base, fuel surcharge, residential) is
rounded to the nearest penny, half up, and the shipment total is the sum of its rounded
components.

5.2 The Shipper may dispute any invoiced amount that exceeds the amount due under this
Agreement.
