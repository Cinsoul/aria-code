# Fulfilment Services Agreement — Schedule 2: Service Levels

**Provider:** Northgate Distribution (HK) Ltd · **Client:** Lumen Cosmetics Ltd
**Monthly service fee:** HKD 120,000

## 1. Dispatch SLA

1.1 An order **received by 14:00** (Hong Kong time) on a Business Day must be dispatched the same
Business Day. An order received **after 14:00** must be dispatched by the end of the next
Business Day.

1.2 An order received on a day that is not a Business Day is treated as received at 09:00 on the
next Business Day.

1.3 **Business Day** means Monday to Friday, excluding Hong Kong public holidays listed in
Annex A.

## 2. Exclusions

2.1 The following orders are excluded from the SLA calculation altogether:

  (a) orders the Client placed on hold (`client_hold = Y`);
  (b) orders whose dispatch deadline falls on a day on which Typhoon Signal No. 8 or higher, or a
      Black Rainstorm Warning, was in force (Annex B).

## 3. Measurement

3.1 On-time rate = orders dispatched by their deadline ÷ orders not excluded, for the calendar
month, as a percentage to two decimal places.

## 4. Service credits

4.1 If the on-time rate for a month is below target, the Provider credits the Client a
percentage of that month's service fee:

| On-time rate | Credit |
|---|---:|
| 98.50% or above | 0% |
| 97.00% to below 98.50% | 2% |
| 95.00% to below 97.00% | 5% |
| below 95.00% | 10% |

---

**Annex A — public holidays (October 2026):** 1 October (National Day), 19 October (Chung Yeung
Festival).

**Annex B — weather days (October 2026):** 14 October (Typhoon Signal No. 8, all day).
